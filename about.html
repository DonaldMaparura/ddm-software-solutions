Fit

Problems DDM solves

Most projects start with a clear operating friction, not a technology preference.

  • Operations that no longer scale Spreadsheets, email approvals and disconnected tools that slow daily work and hide accountability.
  • Customers or staff without a proper channel People need a secure place to request services, track progress and find information without chasing inboxes.
  • Products that need a real platform Marketplaces, commerce flows and digital products that must work in production, not only as a concept deck.
  • Systems that do not talk to each other Payments, accounting, CRM and internal tools creating rework because data never moves cleanly between them.

Approach

How projects are approached

Technology follows the requirement, users, budget and delivery priorities. Stakeholders review working increments before release.

  1. Understand Clarify the business problem, users, current process and required outcome.
  2. Define Agree on scope, priorities, delivery milestones and commercial terms.
  3. Design Structure the workflows, user experience, architecture and integrations.
  4. Build and validate Develop in reviewable increments and test against the agreed requirements.
  5. Launch and support Deploy the approved system, monitor performance and improve it where required.

Principles

Engineering principles

  • Understand the operating reality Start with how work actually happens before choosing tools or architecture.
  • Make technology decisions deliberately Match stack and structure to risk, timeline and the people who will run the system.
  • Keep scope and responsibilities clear Agreed outcomes, ownership and commercial terms before build accelerates.
  • Build in reviewable increments Ship progress stakeholders can see, test and steer.
  • Test before release Validate against agreed requirements before production goes live.
  • Design for maintenance Structure systems for reliability, access control and long-term upkeep.
  • Support production responsibly Monitor, fix and improve once the software is live.

Delivery responsibilities
