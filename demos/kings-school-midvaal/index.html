Welcome

A school where strong foundations and character grow together.

The King's School Midvaal serves families looking for a private primary school in Meyerton with a personal, values-led approach. The website has been designed to present the school with the polish parents expect from leading South African private schools while keeping the tone local, sincere, and accessible.

From the first enquiry to the first day of class, the experience should feel clear: supportive teachers, a safe learning environment, and a community that wants every child to flourish.
