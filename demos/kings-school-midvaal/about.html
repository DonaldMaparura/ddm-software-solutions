About The School

Rooted in faith, focused on every child's future.

The King's School Midvaal combines a caring school family with the clarity, discipline, and aspiration parents expect from a private primary school.

Mission

To educate children with excellence while nurturing character, confidence, and service.

The school exists to support families with a values-led education that develops the whole child. Academic growth matters, but so do kindness, courage, perseverance, responsibility, and a sense of belonging.

Parents should experience the school as approachable and professional: a place where communication is clear, children are safe, and teachers care deeply about progress.

Principal's Message

Welcome to a school community where children are known.

Choosing a school is one of the most important decisions a parent makes. At The King's School Midvaal, our commitment is to partner with families, build strong foundations, and help every learner grow in wisdom, confidence, and character.
