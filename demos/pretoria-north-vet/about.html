Our Story

About Pretoria North Animal Clinic

A dedicated small animal practice where every pet is treated with the compassion and expertise they deserve.

Caring for Pretoria North's Pets Since Day One

Pretoria North Animal Clinic is a two-veterinarian small animal practice proudly serving the Pretoria North community — including Heatherdale and the Rachel De Beer area.

We built our practice on a simple belief: every pet deserves to be treated like family. That means taking the time to listen to pet parents, explaining diagnoses clearly, and providing gentle, fear-free care at every visit.

Whether you're bringing in a new puppy for their first vaccinations, an elderly cat for a wellness check, or a pet in urgent need — you'll find a warm welcome and expert care waiting for you.
