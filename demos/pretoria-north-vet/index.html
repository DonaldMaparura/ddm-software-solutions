Our Services

Complete Care for Dogs & Cats

From preventive wellness to advanced surgical procedures — we offer comprehensive veterinary services under one roof.

Preventive Care & Vaccinations

Annual wellness exams, puppy & kitten vaccinations, parasite prevention, and health screenings to keep your pet protected.

Surgery & Procedures

Spay and neuter, soft tissue surgery, wound care, and post-operative monitoring in a safe, sterile environment.

Emergency Care

Urgent medical attention when your pet needs it most. Call us immediately for emergency situations.

Dental Care

Professional dental cleanings, extractions, and oral health assessments to prevent pain and disease.

Wellness & Nutrition

Weight management, senior pet care, dietary advice, and holistic wellness plans tailored to your pet.

Pet Advice & Consultations

Behaviour guidance, new pet consultations, and ongoing support — we're always happy to answer your questions.

About Us

Your Neighbourhood Veterinary Family

Welcome to Pretoria North Animal Clinic — a dedicated two-veterinarian small animal practice serving the Pretoria North community, including Heatherdale and the Rachel De Beer area.

We believe every pet deserves personalised, compassionate care. Whether it's a routine wellness check, a vaccination, dental work, or an emergency — we're here with expertise, empathy, and a genuine love for animals.
