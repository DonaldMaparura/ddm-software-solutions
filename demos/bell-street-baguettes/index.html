Our Story

Henley's Favourite Sandwich Shop

For years, Bell Street Baguettes has been a beloved part of Henley-on-Thames — a friendly local spot where fresh ingredients meet honest, homemade flavour.

Every morning we prepare our baguettes, sandwiches, salads and savoury favourites using quality ingredients, ready for your quick takeaway lunch.

Whether you're grabbing breakfast on the go, picking up a chicken baguette, or treating yourself to a homemade scotch egg, you'll always find a warm welcome and food made fresh that day.
