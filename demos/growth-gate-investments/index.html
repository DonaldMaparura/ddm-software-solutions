Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers below. Still stuck? Drop us a voice note — sometimes that's faster than typing specs.

How do I place an order? +

WhatsApp +263 783 233 614, or use the quote form. A short list (item, qty, town) is enough to start — photos of nameplates help on machines.

What areas in Zimbabwe do you deliver to? +

Major towns we run weekly; further out we quote courier or your nominated transport. Ask — we've moved kit to places maps barely label.

Do you handle bulk and wholesale orders? +

Yes — that's most of what we do. Bigger lines usually mean sharper landed pricing; say MOQ if you have one.

How do you keep pricing sharp? +

We quote from several sources when it helps, pass through what we save on volume, and tell you when "cheap" would cost you later. No single magic supplier — just homework.

What payment methods do you accept? +

EcoCash, bank transfer, and other routes we agree in writing on the quote. Nothing moves until everyone's clear on the split.

Can you source items not listed on your website? +

Usually. The grid on the site is what we move often — send anything else; we'll say yes, no, or "give us two days to check" honestly.

How long does delivery take? +

Depends what's in stock and how far you are. Local ex-stock often a few working days; imports longer — we put a window on the quote so you're not guessing.

Do you offer after-sales support? +

Ping us if something's wrong on arrival — we'll chase the supplier with you. Warranty terms follow the manufacturer where they apply; we'll spell that out on the quote.
