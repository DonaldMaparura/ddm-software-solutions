Our Story

A Taste of Italy in the Heart of Henley

For over a century, Henley on Klip has been a place of harmony and serenity. At 1904 Trattoria Ponte Vecchio, we honour that spirit with recipes rooted in Italian heritage, prepared with fresh ingredients and served with genuine warmth.

From wood-fired pizza and handmade pasta to celebratory family gatherings and romantic evenings under the stars, every visit is crafted to feel personal, memorable, and unmistakably Italian.
