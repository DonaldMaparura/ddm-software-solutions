Industries

Software shaped around real operating environments.

DDM Software Solutions works with organisations whose digital systems must support concrete operational and customer needs. The company does not claim industry leadership — it focuses on the problems software can solve in each sector.

Property

Agencies and operators often manage listings, tenants and maintenance across disconnected tools.

Typical needs include listings management, CRM, resident portals, maintenance requests and operational dashboards.

Related work: PropService, Liberty Homes

E-commerce and retail

Retailers need product discovery, checkout and payment handling that match how they sell.

Typical needs include catalogues, secure checkout, South African payments, order workflows and brand-led storefronts.

Related work: Elegantlaine

Automotive

Buyers and dealers need a reliable way to discover vehicles and manage listings digitally.

Typical needs include vehicle search, dealer listings, buyer journeys and marketplace administration.

Related work: Hona Marketplace

Financial services

Sensitive processes require controlled access, clear workflows and dependable audit trails.

Typical needs include secure portals, process automation, role-based access and controlled information exchange.

Healthcare

Practices and clinics need clearer enquiry handling, scheduling support and internal administration.

Typical needs include appointment and enquiry flows, service presentation and operational administration tools.

Student accommodation

Operators need structured resident services, applications and facility communication.

Typical needs include applications, resident portals, facility updates and operational administration.

Logistics

Movement of goods depends on visibility, partner coordination and consistent operational data.

Typical needs include tracking visibility, operational dashboards and integrations between systems and partners.

Professional services

Firms need credible digital presence and structured client intake without unnecessary complexity.

Typical needs include service presentation, enquiry conversion, document workflows and client intake systems.
