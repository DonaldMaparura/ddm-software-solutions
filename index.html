FAQ

Questions business owners usually ask first.

How long does a project take?

A focused website or automation can take 2 to 6 weeks. A larger custom system is usually phased over 8 to 16 weeks or more, depending on integrations, data, and approval cycles.

How much does custom software cost?

It depends on scope, risk, integrations, and support needs. After discovery, DDM gives you a practical estimate and can split the work into phases so you do not fund unnecessary features upfront.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Support can include bug fixes, hosting help, monitoring guidance, small improvements, and ongoing development retainers.

Can you improve an existing system?

Yes. DDM can review an existing website, API, backend, database, or cloud setup, then fix performance, reliability, integration, UX, or maintenance issues.

Do you build websites and custom software?

Yes. DDM builds business websites, web applications, internal tools, backend systems, payment integrations, and cloud-hosted software.

What makes DDM different from a general agency?

DDM is positioned around engineering ownership. The emphasis is on direct communication, practical scope, backend reliability, integrations, and support after launch.
