FAQ

Common questions.

How long does a project take?

A focused website or automation project typically takes 2 to 6 weeks. Larger applications and integrations are usually delivered in phases over 8 to 16 weeks or more, depending on scope.

How much does custom software cost?

Cost depends on scope, integrations, risk, and support requirements. We provide a practical estimate after discovery and can phase work so the first release delivers measurable value.

Do you provide support after launch?

Yes. Support includes bug fixes, hosting guidance, monitoring setup, small improvements, and ongoing development retainers where needed.

Can you improve an existing system?

Yes. We review websites, APIs, backends, databases, and cloud deployments — then address performance, reliability, integration, or user experience issues.

What makes DDM different from a general agency?

Engineering ownership, direct communication, and practical delivery. You work with the people building your software, not a sales layer.

Do you work with businesses outside South Africa?

Our primary focus is South African businesses, but we can discuss remote engagements where timezone and communication requirements align.
