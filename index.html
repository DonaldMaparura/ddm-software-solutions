About DDM Software Solutions

Who we are, what we believe, and why clients choose us.

Our Story

DDM Software Solutions was founded in 2018 by a team of senior engineers frustrated with poor-quality software development in the South African market. We started by delivering high-quality products for startups and SMEs, and have since worked with enterprises across fintech, e-commerce, property, agriculture, and healthcare sectors.

Today, we're a 12-person team of engineers, designers, and product specialists based in South Africa, working with clients locally and internationally.
