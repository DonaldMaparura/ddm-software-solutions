When custom software fits

Build when the operation has outgrown generic tools.

Custom development makes sense when the business process is specific, several systems need to work together or important work is being managed through spreadsheets, email and repeated administration.

01

The workflow is specific to the organisation.

Roles, approvals, records and reporting requirements no longer fit an off-the-shelf platform without constant workarounds.

02

Customers or teams need one clear system.

A portal or application can replace disconnected forms, messages and tools with a structured way to complete and track work.

03

Existing systems need to exchange reliable data.

APIs, payment services, accounting tools and internal platforms need controlled integrations instead of repeated capture and manual reconciliation.

Project questions

What affects scope, cost and delivery?

What affects the cost of custom software?

Cost is shaped by the number of workflows and user roles, integrations, data migration, security requirements, reporting, testing and production-support needs. DDM defines these factors before recommending a delivery approach.

How long does a custom software project take?

The timeline depends on scope and integration complexity. A focused first release is planned around the smallest useful operational outcome, with milestones agreed before development begins.

Can DDM improve or connect an existing system?

Yes. The requirement may involve extending an existing application, connecting current tools through APIs or replacing one weak part of the process without rebuilding everything.

Does DDM work across South Africa?

Yes. DDM is based in Gauteng and plans, reviews and supports projects across South Africa through remote delivery and clear review milestones.
